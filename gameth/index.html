How to play

The idea. Every cell of the town has hungry people. Each diner picks the restaurant with the best value — tasty enough, cheap enough, close enough — or stays home and cooks. You choose where to set up, how much to charge, and how fancy to be.

  • Move — click a cell to relocate your truck. The coloured wash is the territory you capture.
  • Price & quality — use the sliders on your card. Higher tiers cost more to run but pull pickier diners.
  • Delivery — the dashed ring is your delivery reach. It widens your market but eats margin (commission + logistics).
  • Loyalty — the habit slider sets how much of yesterday’s custom sticks. High habit rewards getting there first.
  • The centre — downtown maximises foot-traffic and volume, but rent bites. A real volume-vs-margin call.
  • Watch out — leave a big patch under-served for too long and a chain muscles in.

Why & what next. The side panel always explains what is driving your result and offers concrete moves — every hint is simulated before it is shown, so the gain is real.

Nash check. After each day we test the map: if no one can profit by moving or repricing, you’ve hit a Nash equilibrium. Otherwise we show who’d deviate and where.

Amber is you (A). Teal is your rival (B). Rose is the chain (C).
